Charities, vetted for efficiency

Organizations that turn giving into real impact — and a few good ways to give to them directly.

How we vet

Impact per dollar, not just good intentions

Before a charity appears here, we look at how efficiently it turns donations into outcomes — program spending, transparency, and results. When it earns a place, we give you clear, direct ways to support it. joy.us never handles the money; you give straight to the charity or through a fund you trust.

Our standards

The five checks every charity passes

  1. Registered and in good standing. A 501(c)(3) whose status we confirm against its IRS filings.
  2. At least 75 cents of every dollar goes to programs. Taken from its most recent Form 990.
  3. Independently rated. Four stars from Charity Navigator. Where Charity Navigator hasn’t scored it, it must hold a Candid Gold or Platinum seal plus ECFA or BBB accreditation, and we review its three most recent Form 990 filings and name the years and figures checked on its profile.
  4. No fraud or governance scandal. We search the news for investigations, misuse of funds, and governance failures.
  5. It serves the people we exist for. Its programs, not just its mission statement, serve those born with lifelong developmental or physical conditions, children born into crisis or without family, and the caregivers who carry that weight.

Every profile is rechecked each year. A charity that slips below the bar is suspended until it passes again.
