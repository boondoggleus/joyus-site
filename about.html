What we do

We curate charities that are efficient

joy.us is a mission-driven nonprofit that finds and spotlights charities which use every dollar well — efficient, effective organizations where your giving does the most good. We don't accept or process donations. You give directly to the charity, so joy.us is never in the middle and never takes a cent.

How it works

Vetted for efficiency, so you can give with confidence

Before a charity appears here, we check how well it turns donations into real impact. You get the good without the guesswork — then you choose how to give: straight to the charity, or through a fund like Daffy or Every.org. Either way, the gift goes directly to them, never through us.

Why we exist

Reflection, turned into action

joy.us grew out of a long, documented search — a cross-faith study of what it means to live well and do good by one another. Over time that search settled into a simple conviction: reflection is only worth so much until it becomes action. joy.us is that conviction built outward into the world.

The search itself continues, openly, at chasingrabbits.now. joy.us is where it turns into giving.
