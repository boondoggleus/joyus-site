Sudan can’t wait for the world to notice

Updated November 12, 2026

The story

Sudan’s civil war is in its fourth year. 8.6 million people are displaced inside the country and another 4.6 million have fled across its borders. 33.7 million people need humanitarian aid, about half of them children. This year, two in three child casualties were killed by armed drones. Journalist Janine di Giovanni went to the South Sudan border to report on a war most of the world has stopped looking at.

Read her reporting →

The need

The UN’s 2026 response plan for Sudan has received about 47 percent of what it requires. Agencies can reach just 10 percent of the people who need emergency shelter. Last week an aid official in Khartoum said the trucks arriving in Darfur were the last in their stocks.
